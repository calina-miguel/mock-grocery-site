Weeknight Tomato Rice

Simmer washed rice with blended tomatoes, onions, pepper mix, stock, and thyme until fluffy. Finish with vegetables and a drizzle of oil.

Garri Breakfast Bowl

Soak garri briefly, then top with milk, peanuts, banana, and a little honey. Keep it cold and serve right away.

Plantain & Greens Plate

Fry ripe plantains until golden. Serve with sauteed greens, beans, and a spoonful of pepper sauce.
