Our story

A market built around the foods people miss, love, and cook often.

Ayo Market is a neighborhood-style storefront for shoppers who want African and Caribbean groceries presented with warmth, clarity, and everyday convenience.

What we care about

Good groceries should feel easy to find. We organize familiar staples, fresh produce, frozen proteins, and cooking bundles so customers can plan meals quickly.

How we choose

  • Products that belong in real home kitchens
  • Clear descriptions and honest mock pricing
  • Helpful service touches inspired by local grocery shops
